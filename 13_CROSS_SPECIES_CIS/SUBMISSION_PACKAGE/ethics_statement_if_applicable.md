# Ethics Statement / Data-Use Note

**Status: NOT APPLICABLE in the traditional sense — secondary analysis of
publicly distributed, already-published human data.** Confirmed by the
author, 2026-09-26: no IRB approval or ethics-exemption reference is
required for this secondary use of public data, and none is inserted here.

- All human data are from the AOMIC-ID1000 dataset (OpenNeuro ds003097;
  connectome derivative Zenodo 19796783), which was collected, consented,
  and published by the original AOMIC investigators (Snoek et al. 2021 and
  the derivative record's authors). All 900 subjects are anonymized
  (IDs sub-0001..sub-1000 with 100 exclusions upstream by AOMIC QC).
- No new human data were collected; no identifiable data are present or
  derivable in this repository; no ethics re-approval is claimed or required
  for secondary analysis under the dataset's CC-BY-4.0 license.
- The fly connectome (FAFB v783) is a publicly released, anonymized
  connectome resource (CC BY-NC 4.0) used in frozen-artifact form only.
- If the target journal requests an institutional secondary-data
  declaration, the author will supply it in the journal submission system
  (correspondence details are likewise withheld from this public repository).
